I am **Valerio Schiavoni**, Professeur titulaire and Maître d’enseignement et de recherche (MER) in the Complex Systems Group at the University of Neuchâtel.

I coordinate the [CUSO Inter-University Doctoral Program in Computer Science](https://informatique.cuso.ch) for 2025–2026. I also serve as Vice-Chair of the [IFIP TC 6.1 Working Group](https://ifip.informatik.uni-hamburg.de/ifip/tc/6/wg/6.1/officers) and DAIS Steering Chair.

## Academic service

### Leadership and coordination

I serve as Vice-Chair of the [IFIP TC 6.1 Working Group](https://ifip.informatik.uni-hamburg.de/ifip/tc/6/wg/6.1/officers) and DAIS Steering Chair. [DAIS 2027](https://www.discotec.org/2027/dais) will take place in Augsburg, Germany.

I coordinate the [CUSO Inter-University Doctoral Program in Computer Science](https://informatique.cuso.ch) for 2025–2026, and previously held this role from 2019 to 2021. I was also coordinator of the Competence Centre in [Complex Systems and Big Data](http://www2.unine.ch/centres-of-excellence/cms/op/edit/lang/fr/pid/35186).

### Steering committees

- [Symposium on Reliable Distributed Systems (SRDS)](https://srds-conference.org/index.php/steering-committe/) — since 2026.
- [ACM/IFIP/USENIX International Middleware Conference](https://middleware-conf.github.io/steering/) — since 2025.
- [Distributed and Event-Based Systems (DEBS)](https://debs.org/steering-committee/) — since 2025.
- [International Federated Conference on Distributed Computing Techniques (DisCoTec)](https://www.discotec.org/) — since 2025, chair for DAIS.
